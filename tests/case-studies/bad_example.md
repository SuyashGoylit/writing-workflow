## About the client

Based in the Middle East, the client ranks among the region's major petrochemical manufacturers, turning oil and gas feedstock into industrial products. Years ago it rolled out an on-premises SAP ERP platform that digitized core operations and made service delivery more consistent.

Over time, running both its production and disaster recovery environments on-premises in parallel became a growing operational hassle. The company began looking for a data center exit strategy for its DR workloads specifically, one that would be scalable, OpEx-driven, and fully managed without fail.

## The challenge

*Tick the ones that sound familiar. We’ll tailor the conversation.*

**Three gaps in the on-premises DR setup made the case for a data center exit.**

☐ **A DR footprint that does not run in parallel on premises.**

Two full environments, production and DR, ran side by side on dedicated hardware next to the core SAP ERP platform. Sustaining both drained budget and staff time and left little headroom for growth. The scope spanned more than 80 business critical applications plus a multi-terabyte HANA database.

☐ **A scalable, OpEx-driven recovery environment.**

Owning the DR site on premises meant fixed capacity and a fixed cost base. The business wanted recovery capacity that could grow with demand and be funded as an operating expense, so the exit plan was scoped around the DR workloads.

☐ **A DR environment that is fully managed without fail.**

DR has to work every time — the business cannot optimise around failures.

☐ **A partner that can run DR for the long term.**

Disaster recovery protects day-to-day operations and the SAP-driven ERP platform, so any gap in coverage was unacceptable. The company needed a partner to run the environment end to end under a managed model it could depend on.

## The solution

**Cloud4C moved the full DR landscape to Azure, then wrapped backup, security, email protection and regular drills around it.**

### Migrated the DR landscape to a local, compliant Azure cloud.

With its Factory approach, Cloud4C shifted SAP, HANA and the rest of the DR estate onto a compliant Azure cloud hosted locally. The move ran with zero disruption and zero data loss, and the company left its legacy data center behind.

### Automated the backup process.

Automated backup tooling now spans the whole DR landscape. Backups follow one consistent process instead of waiting on manual intervention, which gives the company a backup routine it can depend on.

### Strengthened security with native tooling.

Advanced native security, including Microsoft Defender and AWS GuardDuty with 247 monitoring, now surrounds the workloads running on Azure and raises the security posture of the whole cloud environment.

### Extended DR to Microsoft Exchange.

Cloud4C set up Exchange DR on Azure, so email and communication systems are recovered under the same framework as every other workload. Messaging no longer sits outside the recovery plan as a separate risk.

### Proved recovery through periodic DR drills.

Cloud4C runs DR drills at regular intervals, so the continuity plan is exercised instead of taken on trust. Each drill confirms the recovery environment works before a real incident needs it.

## The results

- **Zero disruption, zero data loss.** The DR landscape moved to Azure without interrupting operations or losing data.
- **Continuity for 80+ applications.** Continuity is secured for 80+ business critical applications.
- **Frictionless HANA operations.** Data operations run smoothly across a 3 TB HANA database, up to 40% faster.
- **Non-SAP assets on cloud.** Non-SAP assets were brought onto cloud for consolidated operations and continuity.
- **OpEx-driven DR on Azure.** The entire DR ecosystem runs on Azure under an OpEx model that keeps expenses controlled.
- **Risk-proof foundation.** A risk-proof base now supports the company’s next-gen initiatives.

**Looking ahead.** The organization now runs a fully managed Azure environment with native security and no longer maintains two on-premises environments side by side. A dependable DR setup that spans SAP, HANA and non-SAP assets gives it a stable base for digital expansion. In an industry where downtime carries heavy operational and financial costs, Cloud4C's experts supported the business well beyond the DR function.
