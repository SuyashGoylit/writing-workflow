## About the client

The client is a major petrochemical manufacturer in the Middle East, part of the region’s oil and gas-derived manufacturing sector. Years earlier, the company deployed an SAP-powered ERP platform on premises to digitize core operations and bring more consistency to service delivery.

As the business scaled, running production and disaster recovery side by side on premises became a growing operational strain. The company began looking for a way out of its data centre, starting with the DR workloads.

## The challenge

*Tick the ones that sound familiar. We’ll tailor the conversation.*

**Three voids in the on-premises DR setup made the case for a data centre exit.**

☐  **A DR footprint that does not run in parallel on premises.**

Production and disaster recovery each ran on their own infrastructure, alongside a core SAP ERP platform. Keeping both alive tied up resources, added cost and left little room to scale. The scope was large, with 80+ business critical applications and a HANA database running into terabytes.

☐  **A scalable, OpEx-driven recovery environment.**

An on-premises setup meant fixed capacity and a fixed cost structure. Leadership wanted DR that could grow with the business and be paid for as an operating expense. The exit strategy was designed for DR workloads specifically.

☐  **A DR environment that is fully managed without fail.**

Because DR protects core operations and the SAP-driven ERP platform, the environment had to be dependable at all times. The company needed a managed model it could rely on.

## The solution

**Cloud4C moved the full DR landscape to Azure, then wrapped backup, security, email protection and regular drills around it.**

### Migrated the DR landscape to a local, compliant Azure cloud.

Cloud4C’s Factory approach carried the full DR landscape, covering SAP, HANA and beyond, to a locally hosted, fully compliant Azure cloud. The petrochemical major exited its legacy data centre completely, with operations protected throughout the move.

### Automated the backup process.

Automated backup solutions now span the DR landscape. Backups follow a consistent process instead of depending on manual intervention.

### Strengthened security with native tooling.

Advanced native security, including Microsoft Defender, strengthens the security posture around the workloads running on cloud.

### Extended DR to Microsoft Exchange.

Exchange DR was set up on Azure, so email and communication infrastructure sit under the same framework as every other system. Nothing is left as a separate, unaddressed risk.

### Proved recovery through periodic DR drills.

Cloud4C organizes periodic drills, so continuity plans are tested on an ongoing basis rather than assumed to work. The recovery environment is regularly proven.

## The results

- **Zero disruption, zero data loss.** The DR landscape moved to Azure without interrupting operations or losing data.

- **Continuity for 80+ applications.** Seamless continuity is secured for 80+ business critical applications.

- **Frictionless HANA operations.** Data operations run smoothly across a 3 TB HANA database.

- **Non-SAP assets on cloud.** Non-SAP assets were brought onto cloud for consolidated operations and continuity.

- **OpEx-driven DR on Azure.** The entire DR ecosystem runs on Azure under an OpEx model that keeps expenses controlled.

- **Risk-proof foundation.** A risk-proof base now supports the company’s next-gen initiatives.

**Looking ahead.** The organization has transitioned to a fully managed Azure environment with native security, eliminating the need to maintain two on-premises environments simultaneously. Having a reliable disaster recovery setup that includes SAP, HANA, and non-SAP assets provides a solid foundation for the organization to expand digitally. Cloud4C professionals provided support beyond the DR function, which is crucial in industries with significant operational and financial impacts.
