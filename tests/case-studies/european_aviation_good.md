## About the client

The client is a European aviation services company that leads the global market for aircraft maintenance, repair and overhaul. Its operations depend on accurate, timely data to keep materials ready, supply chains visible and aircraft available.

To modernize enterprise analytics, the firm set out to move critical data out of its on-prem Oracle systems and into Azure Synapse Analytics, with Material Management and operational reporting as the priority. It also needed a stable platform that could scale for order tracking and efficient inventory management, but recurring pipeline failures and data quality problems put that plan at risk.

## The challenge

*Tick the ones that sound familiar. We’ll tailor the conversation.*

**Five problems in the analytics pipeline were undermining trust in the data.**

☐ **Pipelines that stay stable under high-volume loads.**

Transfers of large data volumes from on-prem Oracle systems into Azure Synapse Analytics failed again and again. Each failure delayed data, weakened confidence in the pipelines and created rework for the teams running Material Management and reporting workflows.

☐ **Clean handling of Oracle source anomalies.**

Invalid date values in the Oracle backend disrupted processing across critical pipelines. Unless these known issues were contained at the source, they would keep triggering reload failures, data gaps and inaccurate reports.

☐ **Complete Material Management data.**

The legacy incremental logic did not capture record updates consistently, which left partial reloads and gaps in Material Management-critical tables. Reporting on inventory accuracy, order lifecycles and operational decisions became harder to rely on.

☐ **Reconciliation that business users can trust.**

During UAT (User Acceptance Testing), business users found reconciliation mismatches that dented their confidence in the cloud analytics platform. Without transparent, explainable validation, adoption of the modernized platform slowed.

☐ **Predictable performance at a controlled cost.**

Slow pipeline runs, Power BI refresh issues and concerns over Synapse SQL Pool costs added pressure. The firm needed the environment stabilized for reliability and also for performance, predictable operations and cost efficiency.

## The solution

**Cloud4C took a DataOps-led approach that put reliability and business trust first, then tuned the Azure Synapse environment for performance and cost.**

### Stabilized the Material Management pipelines.

Cloud4C started with the Azure Synapse pipelines that Material Management depends on, cutting recurring failures so that data from on-prem Oracle systems now reaches Azure more predictably and dependably.

### Corrected SQL logic and schema mismatches.

The team found and fixed SQL logic errors and schema mismatches that were hurting data completeness and accuracy. Key datasets regained their integrity, and downstream reports now draw on consistent, validated data.

### Introduced new incremental load and merge logic.

New incremental load and merge logic improves how record updates are captured, so refreshed datasets reflect the latest available operational information instead of partial reloads.

### Contained known Oracle anomalies.

Controlled data quality handling now deals with known Oracle backend anomalies such as invalid date values. Source-side issues disrupt the pipelines less often, and ingestion and processing are more resilient.

### Consolidated and reconciled over 30 production pipelines.

Cloud4C consolidated and stabilized over 30 production pipelines and ran large-scale historical reloads with full reconciliation. Business users could validate the data more clearly and regained confidence in the modernized environment.

### Optimized Azure Synapse for performance and cost.

Cloud4C right-sized the Azure Synapse Dedicated SQL Pool and tuned its performance. Both execution time and SQL Pool spend came down, and Power BI refreshes became more reliable for business reporting.

## The results

- **Zero unplanned failures.** No unplanned pipeline failures have occurred since stabilization.
- **Data integrity restored.** Integrity is restored across Material Management-critical datasets.
- **Shorter pipeline runs.** Pipeline execution time fell by approximately 40%.
- **Lower SQL Pool costs.** Synapse SQL Pool costs dropped by approximately 40%.
- **Reliable Power BI refreshes.** Power BI refresh reliability improved for business reporting.
- **Faster UAT sign-off.** Business users reached UAT confidence and signed off sooner.

**Looking ahead.** With a stable Azure Synapse foundation in place, the firm is better placed to support dependable Material Management reporting, clearer supply chain visibility and sounder operational decisions. Better completeness, steadier pipelines, transparent reconciliation and stronger reporting performance are rebuilding confidence in cloud-based analytics.
