## Near Zero Downtime, Zero Data Loss: Middle East Petrochemical Major's Data Center Exit to Azure

Cloud4C helped a leading Middle East petrochemical manufacturer exit its legacy on-prem data center and move its entire disaster recovery environment to a locally hosted, fully compliant Azure cloud, with zero disruption and zero data loss.

## A Petrochemical Manufacturer Confronting the Limits of On-Prem DR

The client is a major petrochemical manufacturer in the Middle East, part of the region's broader oil and gas-derived manufacturing sector. The company had earlier deployed an SAP powered ERP platform on-premises to digitize its core operations and bring more consistency to how it delivered services. Over time, running both its production and disaster recovery environments on-premises in parallel became a growing operational hassle. The company began looking for a data center exit strategy for its DR workloads specifically, one that would be scalable, OpEx-driven, and fully managed without fail. That search is where Cloud4C's engagement began.

## The Business Challenge: Making a Case for Exiting a Dual On-Prem Footprint

## Sustaining Production and DR Environments in Parallel On Premise

Maintaining both the production and disaster recovery environments on-premises had become a hassle for the company. Keeping two full environments running side by side, alongside a core ERP platform built on SAP, meant the DR setup was no longer sustainable in its existing form.

## Needing a Scalable, OpEx-Driven Exit Strategy

The company was not just looking to move its DR workloads off legacy infrastructure. It specifically wanted the new environment to be scalable and OpEx-driven, rather than tied to the fixed capacity and cost structure of an on-prem setup.

## Requiring a Fully Managed Model It Could Rely On

Alongside scalability and cost flexibility, the company needed its DR environment to be fully managed without fail, given how central it was to protecting the business's core operations and its SAP-driven ERP platform.

## The Cloud4C Solution: A Factory-Led DR Migration to a Fully Managed Azure DR Environment

## Moving the Entire DR Landscape to Azure Through Cloud4C's Factory Approach

Cloud4C used its Factory approach to transition the client's entire DR landscape to a locally hosted, fully compliant Azure cloud environment. The migration covered the company's core SAP and HANA workloads and was carried out at zero disruption and zero data loss, allowing the business to exit its legacy data center entirely without putting ongoing operations or continuity at risk during the transition itself.

## Building a Layered Protection Model Around Backup, Security, and Email Continuity

Once on Azure, Cloud4C integrated automated backup solutions across the DR landscape to give the company a consistent, dependable backup process rather than one dependent on manual intervention. This was paired with advanced native security, including Microsoft Defender, to strengthen the security posture around the company's workloads on cloud. Cloud4C also extended the same DR approach to Microsoft Exchange, setting up Exchange DR on Azure so that email and communication infrastructure was covered under the same framework as the rest of the environment, rather than left as a separate, unaddressed risk.

## Validating and Operating the Environment for Long-Term Sustainability

To keep the environment dependable over time, Cloud4C organizes periodic DR drills, ensuring the company's continuity plans are tested on an ongoing basis rather than assumed to work. The entire DR ecosystem now runs on Azure cloud under an OpEx model, which ensures business sustainability while keeping expenses controlled, giving the company a recovery environment that is both regularly proven and financially aligned with how the business operates.
