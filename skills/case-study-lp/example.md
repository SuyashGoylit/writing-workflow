## About the client

Based in the Middle East, the client ranks among the region's major petrochemical manufacturers and belongs to its oil and gas-derived manufacturing sector. Years earlier it rolled out an on-premises SAP ERP platform that digitized core operations and made service delivery more consistent.

Growth exposed the cost of that setup. Production and disaster recovery each lived on their own on-premises infrastructure, and running both in parallel became a growing operational strain. The company set out to find a way to exit the data center that applied to its DR workloads specifically.

## The challenge

*Tick the ones that sound familiar. We’ll tailor the conversation.*

**Three gaps in the on-premises DR setup made the case for a data center exit.**

☐ **A way out of running production and DR in parallel on premises.**

Two full environments, production and DR, ran side by side next to the core SAP ERP platform. Sustaining both tied up resources and added cost, with little room to scale. Any exit strategy needed to handle more than 80 business critical applications and a multi-terabyte HANA database.

☐ **A scalable, OpEx-driven recovery environment.**

An on-premises DR site tied the company to fixed capacity and a fixed cost structure. It wanted the new recovery environment to scale with the business and to be funded as an operating expense instead.

☐ **A DR environment that is fully managed without fail.**

Disaster recovery protected the company's core operations and its SAP-driven ERP platform, so the company needed the environment to be fully managed without fail. This requirement sat alongside its goals for scalability and cost flexibility.

## The solution

**Cloud4C moved the full DR landscape to Azure, then wrapped backup, security, email protection and regular drills around it.**

### Migrated the DR landscape to a local, compliant Azure cloud.

Cloud4C applied its Factory approach to move the entire DR landscape, including the core SAP and HANA workloads, onto a compliant Azure cloud hosted locally. The move ran with zero disruption and zero data loss, and the company left its legacy data center entirely.

### Automated the backup process.

Automated backup solutions now run across the DR landscape. Backups follow one consistent process instead of waiting on manual intervention, which gives the company a backup routine it can depend on.

### Strengthened security with native tooling.

Advanced native security, including Microsoft Defender, now surrounds the company's workloads on Azure and strengthens the security posture around them.

### Extended DR to Microsoft Exchange.

Cloud4C set up Exchange DR on Azure, so email and communication systems are covered by the same DR framework as the rest of the environment. Messaging is no longer left outside it as a separate, unaddressed risk.

### Proved recovery through periodic DR drills.

Cloud4C organizes DR drills at regular intervals, so the company's continuity plans are exercised on an ongoing basis instead of being taken on trust. This keeps the recovery environment dependable over time.

## The results

- **Zero disruption, zero data loss.** The DR landscape moved to Azure without interrupting operations or losing data.
- **Continuity for 80+ applications.** Continuity is secured for 80+ business critical applications.
- **Frictionless HANA operations.** Data operations run smoothly across a 3 TB+ HANA database.
- **Non-SAP assets on cloud.** Non-SAP assets were brought onto cloud for consolidated operations and continuity.
- **OpEx-driven DR on Azure.** The entire DR ecosystem runs on Azure under an OpEx model that keeps expenses controlled.
- **Risk-proof foundation.** A risk-proof base now supports the company’s next-gen initiatives.

**Looking ahead.** The organization now runs its DR on a fully managed Azure environment with native security, tested through periodic drills, and has left behind the strain of keeping two on-premises environments running side by side. A stable DR setup that spans SAP, HANA and non-SAP assets gives it a steadier base as its digital footprint grows. Any disruption in this industry carries operational and financial weight, and the groundwork Cloud4C's experts laid matters well beyond the DR function itself.
