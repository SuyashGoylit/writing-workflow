## Pitch format
- H2 heading tied to the topic. Her published patterns include "How Cloud4C Supports [Audience/Outcome]", "Cloud4C: [Positioning Line]" and "Cloud4C's [Offering] for [Outcome]".
- **Paragraph 1:** what Cloud4C does for this audience, anchored on the Self-Healing Operations Platform (linked) and single-SLA or single-provider accountability. Add a few scale facts where they help.
- **Paragraph 2:** the 2-4 most relevant solutions, each tied to a problem the blog raised, with 1-2 of them linked.
- **Optional final line:** a one-sentence bold CTA link, such as `[**Contact Cloud4C experts**](https://www.cloud4c.com/contact-us) to get an assessment of …`.
- Keep it factual. The no-hype and no-em-dash rules still apply. Third person throughout, so write "Cloud4C" or "the platform", never "we", "our" or "us". (Some published pitches use "we". Keep to third person unless the writer says the client prefers it.)
